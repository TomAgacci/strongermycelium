============================================================
ICHOR DEFINITIONS
============================================================

Mineral Ichor = SiO₂
Water Ichor   = H₂O + dissolved ions (Ca²⁺, Na⁺, Cl⁻, etc.)

Mineral Ichor (SiO₂) acts as the structural skeleton.
Water Ichor is the hydration + ion transport medium.

============================================================
WHERE TO FIND MINERAL ICHOR (SiO₂) IN REAL ITEMS
============================================================

You can source SiO₂ from common, safe, everyday materials:

1. Play Sand (Home Depot, Lowe’s, hardware stores)
   - Usually 80–95% SiO₂
   - Clean, inert, perfect for mineral skeleton

2. Glass Dust / Crushed Glass
   - Window glass, bottle glass, tempered glass
   - Crush into fine particles (wear PPE)
   - ~70–75% SiO₂

3. Silica Flour
   - Sold for ceramics, pottery, and casting
   - Very fine SiO₂ powder
   - Excellent for high-density composites

4. Aquarium Sand
   - Often pure silica sand
   - Pre-washed, consistent grain size

5. Playground Sandbags
   - Cheap, widely available
   - Mostly silica

Any of these can serve as Mineral Ichor in your block.

============================================================
MATERIALS (PER 1 LITER OF MIX)
============================================================

Mineral Ichor (SiO₂ sand/fines) .......... 0.40–0.60 kg
Lime (hydrated/hydraulic) ................ 0.15–0.25 kg
Water Ichor (H₂O + ions) ................. 0.18–0.22 kg
Salt (NaCl, low %) ........................ 0.005–0.015 kg
Mycelium (spawn) .......................... 0.03–0.06 kg
Organic fines (optional, tiny amount) ..... 1–3% of dry mass

NOTE:
Mycelium cannot grow on pure SiO₂.
You must add a tiny amount of organic fines (rice flour, oat flour, cellulose powder)
to give the mycelium something to colonize.

============================================================
FULL INSTRUCTIONS
============================================================

STEP 1 — PREPARE MINERAL ICHOR SKELETON
- Use SiO₂ sand or silica flour.
- Target a mix of coarse + fine grains for density.
- This replaces hemp shiv entirely.

STEP 2 — ADD WATER ICHOR
- Mix water with dissolved ions:
  - Ca²⁺ from lime
  - Na⁺ / Cl⁻ from salt
- Add enough water to make a damp, packable mineral paste.

STEP 3 — ADD LIME LANE
- Add 15–25% lime by dry mass.
- Lime binds the SiO₂ and forms the mineral matrix.
- Mix thoroughly until uniform.

STEP 4 — ADD MYCELIUM LANE
- Add mycelium spawn.
- Add 1–3% organic fines so the mycelium can colonize.
- Mix gently so the network is not destroyed.

STEP 5 — GROWTH PHASE
- Incubate at 22–26°C.
- Humidity 80–95%.
- Grow 5–10 days until the organic fines are colonized.
- Mycelium will NOT colonize SiO₂, but it will bind around it.

STEP 6 — POST-GROWTH MINERAL BOOST
- Add additional lime to reach 20–25% total.
- Add a small amount of salt (ionic tuning).
- Mix lightly.

STEP 7 — LOAD INTO MOLD
- Use a metal mold (300×600×200 mm or your chosen size).
- Vibrate or tap to remove air pockets.

STEP 8 — COMPRESSION LANE
- Apply 20–30 tons of compression.
- Hold 5–20 minutes.
- This is the main driver of MPa strength.

STEP 9 — DRYING LANE
- Dry at 40–60°C for 12–24 hours.
- Removes free water.
- Stops biological activity.

STEP 10 — HEAT-PRESS LANE
- Heat at 120–160°C for 30–90 minutes.
- Apply 10–20 tons compression during heating.
- This locks the mineral matrix and increases MPa.

STEP 11 — CURING LANE
- Cure 7–28 days at 50–70% humidity.
- Lime carbonates and strengthens the block.

============================================================
EXPECTED STRENGTH (NO HEMP VERSION)
============================================================

With SiO₂ skeleton + lime + compression + heat-press:

5–15 MPa  = mid-strength mineral-biocomposite
20–40 MPa = high-strength mineral-dominant composite

Mycelium acts as a micro-binder, not the main structure.

============================================================
NOTES ON SAFETY AND CHEMISTRY
============================================================

- SiO₂ is inert and safe to handle.
- Lime is caustic; wear gloves and eye protection.
- Mycelium is safe but keep environment clean.
- Salt should be kept low to avoid brittleness.
- Avoid reactive sodium oxides/peroxides (not needed).

============================================================
SUMMARY
============================================================

This recipe creates a hemp-free, ichor-based, mineral-biocomposite block using:

Mineral Ichor (SiO₂) = structural skeleton
Water Ichor (H₂O + ions) = hydration + chemistry
Lime Lane = binder
Salt Lane = ionic tuning
Mycelium Lane = micro-binding network

Strength range: 5–40 MPa depending on compression and heat-press.
